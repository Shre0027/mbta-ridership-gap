# Data quality log

Issues found while reconciling the source datasets, and how each was handled.

| # | Issue | Where | Impact | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Same station has two names: **"State"** vs **"State Street"** | ODX stop files vs Gated Station Entries | Station join silently drops the busiest Blue Line station | Normalized to "State" before joining |
| 2 | **Green Line split into branches** (Green Line B/C/D/E) in Fall 2025 only; earlier seasons use one "Green Line" | Fall 2025 stop-level file | Line totals for 2025 miss Green entirely | Rolled branches up to "Green Line" |
| 3 | **Government Center split flipped on March 6, 2019**: entries were split 90% Blue / 10% Green through March 5, then 10% Blue / 90% Green from March 6, with no note in the data or its documentation (which describes 90% Green). State Street stays 10% Blue / 90% Orange throughout | Gated Station Entries | Taps credited to Blue at Government Center drop ~90% overnight; station trends across that date show a collapse that never happened | Reported, not "corrected"; station-level Blue comparisons for 2017–2018 read with this in mind |
| 4 | Field names differ between seasons (`direction_id`/`stop_id` vs `dir_id`/`parent_station`; time periods vs hours) | Stop-level files | Combining seasons needs care | Analysis uses only fields common to all files |
| 5 | Stop-level files exist only for Fall 2017–2019 and 2023–2025 | MBTA Open Data Portal | No comparison possible for 2020–2022 | Years without both datasets are excluded |
| 6 | Gate taps include employee taps and miss held-open gates (special events, emergencies) | Gated Station Entries documentation | Taps are an imperfect proxy | Used only for station-level ratios, not as ridership |
| 7 | **Green Line ungated-stop boardings halve in Fall 2019** (21k vs 44k in Fall 2018; 15% vs 25% of Green Line boardings) with no documented method change | Stop-level (ODX) Fall 2019 | 2019 Green Line gap looks unusually large (+24%) | Reported; not adjusted |
| 8 | **Monthly Green Line factor shifts regime**: monthly riders ÷ gate taps runs mostly 1.7–2.0× from Jul 2023 to Jun 2024, then mostly 2.4–2.8× from Aug 2024; Red/Orange/Blue stay ~1.3–1.45× | Monthly Ridership by Mode and Line | Green Line monthly totals jump ~22% Aug→Sep 2024 independent of taps; drives the Green gap's sign flip | Reported; explained as the cause of the Green swing. Timing coincides with the MBTA's AFC 2.0 "Tap to Ride" launch on Aug 1, 2024 (onboard readers on Green Line trolleys); NBC10/BU reported the share of Green riders boarding at ungated stops rose from ~42–46% to 56.5% in Sep 2024. Not confirmed by OPMI |
| 9 | **"Gated entries" reported at Green Line Extension stations that have no fare gates** (Lechmere, Union Square, East Somerville, Gilman, Magoun, Ball Square, Medford/Tufts: 64–249/day). MBTA says these stations were designed without fare gates; riders validate at fare machines | Gated Station Entries | A naive taps-vs-model ratio shows 3–7× at GLX stations, which looks like a transfer effect but isn't | Treated as ungated; no ratio computed |
| 10 | **Gate-entry split factors misallocate taps between lines at shared stations**: at State, 90% of taps go to Orange and 10% to Blue, but the model and the 2025 rider survey show far more Blue boardings than that implies | Gated Station Entries | Line-level station comparisons at transfer stations are unreliable | Station-level comparisons combine all lines at a station |
| 11 | **Assembly and Wollaston run 1.6× model ÷ taps in Fall 2025** without being transfer stations (both 1.0–1.05× in 2017–19; Assembly 1.30× → 1.51× → 1.61× in 2023–25) | Gated Station Entries vs stop-level | Could be misread as missed transfers | Reported as the high end of the post-COVID non-tap rise; no station-level public data explains it |
| 12 | **Service shutdowns sit inside the fall windows** (e.g. Red Line Braintree branch closed Sep 6–29, 2024; Broadway–North Quincy Nov 5–10, 2024) and are not flagged in either ridership dataset | Monthly Ridership; Gated Station Entries | Monthly fall averages dip in shutdown months (Red: Sep 2024 124,470, Nov 106,335 vs Oct 146,264); naive tap averages count closed days as zero | Station taps averaged over open days only; Red 2024 gap attributed to shutdowns (October alone is within 1.3% of the model) |
